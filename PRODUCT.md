# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated for this concept: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with CDN-loaded enhancement libraries. This keeps the owner presentation portable and inexpensive to revise.

## Users

- Diners in Pérez Zeledón and Bijagua, often browsing from a phone or a table QR code.
- The restaurant owner, who needs to evaluate and request changes to a visual concept.
- Spanish-speaking guests are primary; English-speaking guests require a complete language switch.

## Product Purpose

Present Kingyo Sushi & Fusión as a refined Japanese-fusion restaurant, make verified menu items easy to explore, and shorten the path to WhatsApp, directions and branch information.

## Positioning

Kingyo combines Japanese technique with generous, locally recognizable fusion specialties under the promise “Calidad, Sabor y Tradición”.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first browsing, including QR access at the table. Visitors may dine in, order takeaway or ask about delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish/English interface switch.
- Live menu search, category filters, service-price selector, cart and WhatsApp order handoff.
- Responsive navigation, branch selector and direct contact links.
- Only menu facts visible in the supplied photographs are treated as verified.
- Hours, Bijagua address, social handles and a production URL remain undecided and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Commercial name: Kingyo Sushi & Fusión.
- Slogan: Calidad, Sabor y Tradición.
- Preserve the official circular fish mark and the angular KINGYO wordmark.
- Preserve the black, vermilion, warm gold and Japanese wave/blossom language visible in the physical menu.

## Product Principles

- Put fast mobile decisions before ornamental complexity.
- Let authentic food and brand assets carry the presentation.
- Separate verified facts from information still awaiting the owner.
- Keep the concept easy to revise after the owner presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-visible controls, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, legible mobile type and complete bilingual labels.
